A short history of Batangas coffee

Why Batangas is known for kapeng barako.

Batangas has carried a coffee tradition for generations. Its fertile volcanic soil, warm climate, and highland farms helped Liberica coffee thrive in the province. The beans became known as barako, a Batangueño word associated with a strong, courageous character.

01

Seeds found a home

In the 1800s, coffee trees spread through Lipa and nearby towns. The land and climate gave local farmers ideal conditions for growing fragrant Liberica cherries.

02

Barako became a culture

Families roasted and brewed the bold beans at home. A cup was served to guests, shared after meals, and became part of everyday Batangueño hospitality.

03

A heritage worth keeping

Today, kapeng barako represents Batangas pride: a distinct aroma, full body, and a resilient farming community behind every brew.
